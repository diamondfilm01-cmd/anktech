Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

AnkTech ("we", "our", or "us") operates the App Lock mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Privacy-First Design

App Lock operates with an offline, privacy-first architecture. We do not collect, transmit, rent, or sell your personal data to remote servers.

  • Passcodes, PINs, and Patterns: All passcodes are hashed locally on your device using cryptographic SHA-256 algorithms with random salt values. Plain text credentials are never saved or sent over any network.
  • Biometric Data: Biometric authentication (fingerprint) is managed via official Android BiometricPrompt system APIs. We do not access, process, or store raw biometric records.
  • Installed Applications: The application scans installed packages locally to let you choose apps to protect. This package list remains strictly on your device.

2. Android System Permissions

To provide accurate and real-time app protection, App Lock requires the following permissions:

  • BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE: Used strictly to detect when a locked app is brought to the foreground and immediately present the lock screen. It does not monitor input, read personal messages, or extract on-screen content.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS: Used as an auxiliary fail-safe engine to monitor foreground application transitions.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Required to show the lock screen overlay interface over protected apps.
  • BIND_DEVICE_ADMIN: Protects App Lock against unauthorized removal, uninstallation, or force-stopping by third parties without master passcode entry.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on newer Android versions to run persistent foreground safety services.
  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Required to display the list of installed applications so you can configure app lock rules.

4. Data Storage and Retention

All configurations, locked app selections, and salted credential hashes are stored locally within private application storage (SharedPreferences). No data is synced to external cloud databases.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
