Privacy Policy for InvigiDuty

Effective Date: 2026

InvigiDuty respects and protects user privacy. This policy explains how information is handled, secured, and controlled by you.

1. Data Collection & Local Storage

InvigiDuty operates with an offline-first architecture. All records, schedules, and duty entries are saved directly inside your device's local memory. We do not transmit or sell your personal records to external commercial servers.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

The app uses Google AdMob and Google Play Services to serve advertisements and maintain performance. These services may collect technical device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) in accordance with Google Play Developer policies.

3. Data & Account Deletion Policy

Users maintain complete ownership and control over their data:

  • Self-Service Erasure: Because all records are stored locally on your device, you can permanently erase all application data at any time directly through the app settings or by clearing the app data/cache in Android Settings (Settings > Apps > InvigiDuty > Storage > Clear Data).
  • Assistance & Support: If you face any difficulties or require further assistance regarding data erasure, you can contact us directly at diamondfilm01@gmail.com.
