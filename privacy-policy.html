Privacy Policy for InvigiDuty

Last Updated: 2026

InvigiDuty is dedicated to protecting the privacy of its users. This policy outlines how information is collected, handled, and deleted.

1. Data Collection & Usage

InvigiDuty stores application records and preferences locally on your device or via secure cloud authentication. We do not sell your personal records to third parties.

2. Third-Party Services & Ads

We integrate Google AdMob and Google Play Services to serve advertisements and maintain app health. These services may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID).

3. Account & Data Deletion Policy

Users have complete authority to manage and remove their records:

  • In-App Data Erasure: Open InvigiDuty -> Go to Settings -> Select Clear App Data to permanently wipe stored device information.
  • Manual Account Deletion Request: If you registered an account or cloud profile, send an email to support@invigiduty.com with the subject line "Delete My Account". All associated data will be deleted within 7 business days.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions, reach out to us at: support@invigiduty.com
